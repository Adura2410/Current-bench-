Editorial & Privacy Policy

Current Bench is a news discovery and aggregation project that helps readers find Nigerian and international headlines in one place. This page explains how the feed works and its current limitations.

Important: Current Bench is an independent aggregation project, not the publisher of most linked reporting. The original publisher remains the source of record for each linked story. Feed availability and update timing can vary.

Sources, attribution and reuse

Headlines and short feed summaries are collected from public RSS feeds and configured news-data services. Cards identify the available source and link readers to the publisher. We do not intentionally copy publisher photographs. A feed or API response does not automatically grant permission to republish full articles, images, video or other copyrighted material.

Where content is displayed beyond a headline or short summary, the collector must have a clear licence or permission for that specific use. News Central text may be included only under the source's stated Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 terms; those terms do not automatically cover photographs or third-party material.

Accuracy and corrections

Stories are collected automatically and are not all individually reviewed before appearing. Current Bench may display outdated, incomplete, duplicated or incorrectly categorised feed information. Readers should consult the linked publisher for context and updates. If you spot an error or a broken source link, please contact the site operator through the repository issue tracker: Current Bench issue tracker.

Privacy and analytics

The current static site does not provide user accounts or ask visitors to submit personal details. The site may be served through GitHub Pages, whose infrastructure may process standard technical request data under its own policies. This page does not claim that third-party hosting or linked publishers collect no data. Clicking an external source link takes you to that publisher, under its own privacy policy. No advertising or analytics integration is represented here as active.

Automated updates and football data

A scheduled GitHub Actions workflow attempts to refresh headlines and football fixtures about every three hours. Scheduled jobs can be delayed, APIs can impose quotas, and providers can fail or restrict competitions. Live-score availability is not guaranteed. A match marked scheduled or live may be delayed or incomplete; verify important results with the relevant competition or provider.

Contact and ownership

This project is maintained through its public repository. For corrections, source attribution concerns, privacy questions or removal requests, open an issue at github.com/Adura2410/Current-bench-/issues with the relevant story URL and a concise explanation. Do not post private personal information in a public issue.
